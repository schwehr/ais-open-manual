# Appendix A — Timeline

This appendix consolidates the chronological evolution of the Automatic Identification System (AIS), its technological foundations, institutional governing frameworks, enabling software, and operational applications. It merges the historical milestones recorded in [`schwehr/gis-history`](https://github.com/schwehr/gis-history) (tagged with `⟨H⟩`) with the domain-specific milestones introduced across the sixty-nine chapters of this handbook (tagged with `⟨+⟩`).

Entries are ordered chronologically by year and month. Each event cites its primary provenance—either the underlying `gis-history` chronicle or the corresponding chapter in *The AIS Handbook*. Unconfirmed dates or details preserve the handbook's strict `` flags.
