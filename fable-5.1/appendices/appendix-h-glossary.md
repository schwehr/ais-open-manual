# Appendix H — Glossary and acronyms

This appendix compiles the canonical terms, acronyms, mathematical abbreviations, and operational definitions used throughout *The AIS Handbook*. It aggregates terminology from international conventions, technical recommendations, equipment standards, and maritime administrative codes governing the Automatic Identification System (AIS) and its evolution into the VHF Data Exchange System (VDES).

The appendix is organized into three table-driven sections:
1. **H.1 Acronyms and initialisms:** Standardized acronyms across maritime radiocommunications, navigation, standards development organizations, and regulatory bodies.
2. **H.2 Master glossary of maritime and radiocommunication terms:** Authoritative definitions for technical, RF, link-layer, data-science, and maritime operational terms introduced and bolded across the handbook.
3. **H.3 Disambiguation register ("Definitions that bite"):** Critical pairs and triplets of easily confused concepts whose conflation leads to fatal navigational errors, algorithmic failures, regulatory non-compliance, or analytical bias.

For standards citations and international legal instruments referenced herein, see [Chapter 15](../chapters/ch15-standards-that-define-ais.md), [Chapter 16](../chapters/ch16-laws-and-treaties.md), [Appendix B](../appendices/appendix-b-standards-register.md), and [Appendix J](../appendices/appendix-j-organizations.md). Bit-level message structures are cataloged in [Appendix C](../appendices/appendix-c-message-bit-layouts.md), and numeric code enumerations are compiled in [Appendix D](../appendices/appendix-d-code-tables.md).

| **Synthetic AtoN vs. Virtual AtoN** | A **synthetic AtoN** marks an *actual physical buoy* or structure using transmissions from a remote shore station. A **virtual AtoN** marks a navigational hazard where *no physical marker exists*, appearing purely as a digital symbol on ECDIS. | [Chapter 11](../chapters/ch11-growth-2004-2015.md), [Chapter 20](../chapters/ch20-architecture-and-station-classes.md), [Chapter 22](../chapters/ch22-message-catalog.md), [Chapter 68](../chapters/ch68-special-purpose-ais.md) |
| **Autonomous Maritime Radio Device (AMRD) Group A vs. Group B** | Under ITU-R M.2135, **Group A** AMRDs participate in maritime safety and operate on AIS channels with MMSI format `972...` or `974...`. **Group B** AMRDs (e.g., oceanographic trackers, fishing net buoys) do not participate in safety and are strictly prohibited from transmitting on AIS 1 and AIS 2. | [Chapter 13](../chapters/ch13-mmsi-deep-dive.md), [Chapter 20](../chapters/ch20-architecture-and-station-classes.md), [Chapter 65](../chapters/ch65-hacks-unintended-uses.md), [Chapter 68](../chapters/ch68-special-purpose-ais.md) |
