# `figures/ch12/`

Figure assets for Chapter 12 of "The AIS Handbook", an open, citable handbook
on the maritime Automatic Identification System. This directory currently holds
a single generated SVG: a condensed historical timeline of AIS and the maritime
safety context it grew out of, intended to be embedded in the chapter text.

The figure is a Matplotlib export (v3.11.2, dated 2026-10-04), not a hand-drawn
graphic, so it should be regenerated from its source script rather than edited
by hand when the content changes.

## Files
| File | Summary |
|---|---|
| `timeline.svg` | Matplotlib-generated SVG (516.6 × 314.4 pt) titled "AIS timeline (selected; see Appendix A)". A single horizontal axis spans roughly 1905–2030 with x-ticks every 20 years (1920–2020) and no y-axis. Event labels are rotated 90° and alternate above/below the baseline: 1912 Titanic; 1914 SOLAS; 1989 Exxon Valdez / STDMA patent (SE); 1998 ITU-R M.1371-0; MSC.74(69); 2000 SOLAS V/19 adopted; 2002 in force; MTSA; 9/11 acceleration; 2004 carriage complete (Dec); 2006 Class B (IEC 62287); 2008 first SAT-AIS (NTS); 2010 AISSat-1; libais; Msg 27; 2012 Whale Alert; 2014 Trend Micro ACSAC; 2015 VDES M.2092; A.1106(29); 2016 GFW launch; 2017 Black Sea GNSS spoofing; 2021 China feed drop; 2024 IALA IGO (verify). |

## Notes for agents
- `timeline.svg` is machine-generated by Matplotlib (see the `<metadata>` block). Do not hand-edit the SVG; locate and update the generating script (not in this directory) and re-export instead.
- The title says "(selected; see Appendix A)", so this is a curated subset of events; the authoritative full chronology lives in Appendix A of the handbook. Keep the two consistent if events are added or redated.
- One label is explicitly flagged "2024 IALA IGO (verify)". That date/event has not been confirmed in the figure; verify before relying on it or remove the "(verify)" tag once checked.
- Label text is only 6.5 px and rotated −90°, with alternating up/down leader lines (grey, 0.6 pt) off a 1.5 pt black baseline at y≈155. Densely packed 2000s–2020s labels are already close; adding more events in that range will likely cause overlap.
- The SVG uses the DejaVu Sans font stack with text left as `<text>` elements (not outlined paths), so it renders with whatever fallback fonts the viewer has.
- There are no subdirectories, data files, or scripts here; everything else for Chapter 12 lives elsewhere in the repository.
