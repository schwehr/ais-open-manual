# `book/part2-rf-hardware` — Directory Guide for Agents

## Overview
This directory contains Part II (*Radio Frequency Engineering, Propagation, Hardware, and Collection Networks*, Chapters 5–10) of *Automatic Identification System (AIS): The Open Manual*. It covers the electromagnetics of the maritime VHF band ($156.000\text{–}162.050\text{ MHz}$), numerical propagation and tropospheric ducting models, the ITU-R M.1371-5 physical/link-layer encoding pipeline and IQ packet-recovery algorithms, platform-specific antenna engineering, commercial and Software-Defined Radio (SDR) transceiver hardware, and the design of terrestrial and offshore AIS collection networks.

## Subdirectories
None. This is a leaf directory under `book/`.

## Files in This Directory

- **`ch05-vhf-rf-basics-channels-and-noise.md`**
  - Establishes maritime VHF band physics ($\lambda \approx 1.85\text{ m}$ at $162\text{ MHz}$, vertical linear polarization, $\text{dBi} = \text{dBd} + 2.15\text{ dB}$) and provides the complete ITU Radio Regulations Appendix 18 channel catalog for AIS 1 (`Ch 2087`, $161.975\text{ MHz}$), AIS 2 (`Ch 2088`, $162.025\text{ MHz}$), Long-Range Satellite AIS (`Ch 75` $156.775\text{ MHz}$ & `Ch 76` $156.825\text{ MHz}$), DSC telecommand (`Ch 70`, $156.525\text{ MHz}$), ASM 1/2 (`Ch 2027/2028`), VDES terrestrial/satellite blocks (ITU-R M.2092-1), and AMRD Group B fishing buoys (`Ch 2006`, $160.900\text{ MHz}$, ITU-R M.2135-0).
  - Derives the standard refracted radio horizon ($k = 4/3$, $d_{\text{NM}} \approx 2.23(\sqrt{h_{t,\text{m}}} + \sqrt{h_{r,\text{m}}})$), Fresnel zone clearance, complex seawater permittivity ($\epsilon_c \approx 80 - j499$), and the Two-Ray sea-surface reflection model, proving how destructive multipath nulls ($d_{\text{null},k} = \frac{2 h_t h_r}{k\lambda}$), the breakpoint distance ($d_{\text{bp}} = \frac{4 h_t h_r}{\lambda}$), and asymptotic $1/d^4$ ($40\text{ dB/decade}$) roll-off govern link range.
  - Analyzes shipboard electromagnetic interference (EMI) and co-site receiver desensitization: unshielded PWM LED navigation lights (USCG Marine Safety Alert 13-18 / IEC 60945 notch), VFDs/thrusters, $25\text{ W}$ VHF voice radio LNA blocking ($+22\text{ dBm}$ at $2\text{ m}$ horizontal vs. vertical dipole separation), and Rusty Bolt Passive Intermodulation (PIM), concluding with a runnable Python Two-Ray & LED EMI link-budget simulator.

- **`ch06-rf-propagation-ducting-and-loading.md`**
  - Formulates five classes of $162\text{ MHz}$ propagation models: Free-Space Path Loss (FSPL), Curved-Earth Rough-Sea Two-Ray (incorporating spherical divergence $D$ and Ament/Miller-Brown rough-sea scattering $\rho_s$), Longley-Rice Irregular Terrain Model (ITM) with Bullington/Fresnel-Kirchhoff knife-edge diffraction $J(\nu)$ (explaining why $162\text{ MHz}$ AIS diffracts around river bends and fjords where $9.4\text{ GHz}$ X-band radar is blocked), ITU-R P.1546-6 & P.528-5, and the 2D Split-Step Fourier Parabolic Equation (SSPE) used in US Navy APM/AREPS and TEMPER.
  - Examines tropospheric refractivity $N$ and modified refractivity $M(z) = N(z) + 0.157 z_{\text{m}}$, waveguide cutoff criteria ($\Delta z_d \sqrt{\Delta M} \gtrsim 737$) for evaporation, surface-based, and elevated ducts ($300\text{–}1,500+\text{ NM}$ over-the-horizon reception), global Hepburn ducting hotspots, and opportunistic inversion of AIS TAG-block RSSI observations to benchmark NWP models (ERA5, GFS, HRRR, COAMPS).
  - Synthesizes **ITU-R Report M.2287-0** and peer-reviewed studies (Eriksen et al., Hoye et al., Cervera et al., Last et al.) on VDL slot loading ($4,500\text{ slots/min}$), explaining why SOTDMA triggers intentional **cell shrinking** above $50\%$ loading, and provides a runnable Python 2D SSPE solver (`scipy.fft.dst`) and SOTDMA cell-shrinking Monte Carlo simulator.

- **`ch07-physical-layer-encoding-and-packet-recovery.md`**
  - Walks through the complete ITU-R M.1371-5 Annex 2 physical and data-link layer pipeline for a $26.667\text{ ms}$ (256-bit) time slot at $9,600\text{ bps}$: 8-bit PA ramp-up, 24-bit training preamble (`0101...`), HDLC `0x7E` Start Flag, the MSB-first logical field vs. LSB-first per-byte over-the-air serialization duality, 16-bit CRC-CCITT ($G(x) = x^{16} + x^{12} + x^5 + 1$, `0xFFFF` preset, ones'-complement output), HDLC zero-bit stuffing after five consecutive `1`s, `0x7E` End Flag, 24-bit buffer breakdown ($202.3\text{ NM}$ delay budget), NRZI differential encoding, and GMSK modulation ($h = 0.5$, $BT = 0.4$ TX / $0.5$ RX).
  - Details four forensic packet-salvage algorithms for corrupted or collided baseband IQ recordings: (1) Coherent Laurent/Viterbi demodulation and pre-unstuffing soft-LLR syndromic Chase-2 bit-flipping (with MMSI/kinematic plausibility gating), (2) Prior-aided local MMSI & SOTDMA slot-reservation header constraints, (3) Partial-packet extraction from tail-collided bursts (showing the full 30-bit MMSI arrives within $3.96\text{ ms}$ and full WGS84 Lat/Lon within $12.5\text{ ms}$ after the Start Flag), and (4) Iterative Successive Interference Cancellation (SIC) and Joint MLSE for co-channel collisions.
  - Includes a complete runnable Python implementation of the AIS physical/link layer, soft-syndrome 2-bit error recovery, and two-ship SIC separation.

- **`ch08-antenna-engineering-and-selection.md`**
  - Derives the vertical radiation pattern $E_N(\psi) = F_{\text{elem}}(\psi) \cdot AF_N(\psi)$ of $\frac{1}{4}\lambda$, $\frac{1}{2}\lambda$, $\frac{5}{8}\lambda$, and $N$-element stacked collinear arrays at $162.0\text{ MHz}$, proving the fundamental conservation-of-energy trade-off between horizon directivity ($D_N$) and vertical Half-Power Beamwidth ($\text{HPBW}_{\text{el}} \approx \frac{50.8^\circ}{N(d/\lambda)}$).
  - Exposes recreational marine antenna marketing inflation ("$3\text{ dB}$/$6\text{ dB}$/$9\text{ dB}$" labels vs. true $\text{dBi}$/$\text{dBd}$) and quantifies the $+3.32\%$ detuning VSWR penalty ($\text{VSWR} \approx 2.6:1\text{–}4.5:1$, PA ALC foldback, and collinear beam squint) when operating $162.0\text{ MHz}$ AIS on legacy $156.8\text{ MHz}$ (Channel 16) voice whips.
  - Provides platform-specific antenna recommendations and physical justifications for: (1) Large SOLAS ships ($2.15\text{–}5.0\text{ dBi}$ DC-grounded commercial fiberglass dipoles/collinears, IMO MSC.1/Circ.1252 co-site rules), (2) Sailboats & small craft (proving why a $2.15\text{ dBi}$ $\frac{1}{2}\lambda$ whip with $78.1^\circ$ HPBW outperforms a $9\text{ dBi}$ collinear by $>18\text{ dB}$ at $25^\circ$ heel; active zero-loss IEC 62287-1/2 VHF/AIS splitters), (3) Very small systems (AIS-MOB lifejacket beacons using saltwater capacitive counterpoise and $+3\text{–}5\text{ MHz}$ dry-air pre-offset, UAV conformal blades, and LEO CubeSat turnstile/diversity arrays combating ionospheric Faraday rotation $\Delta\Omega \propto \text{TEC}/f^2$), and (4) Rigid shore stations (exposed folded-dipole arrays, 5–8 element Yagis, low-loss LMR-400/Heliax coax, and Filter-Before-LNA topology).
  - Includes a runnable Python script modeling horizon gain across $0^\circ\text{–}40^\circ$ heel/roll angles and $30\text{ m}$ coaxial feedline + VSWR loss budgets.

- **`ch10-shore-and-sea-collection-networks.md`**
  - Analyzes terrestrial site selection across lighthouses, port VTS towers, high-rise roofs, grain elevators (NFPA 70 Class II explosive dust rules), and coastal mountains, including NFPA 780 / Motorola R56 lightning grounding and 3-layer butyl/vinyl connector weatherproofing.
  - Proves the **"Too High" Elevation Paradox**: an omnidirectional $9\text{ dBi}$ collinear on a $1,000\text{ m}$ coastal mountain ($d_{\text{NM}} \approx 80.5\text{ NM}$) pulls in $M_{\text{cells}} \approx 5.3$ independent $35\text{ NM}$ SOTDMA cells simultaneously (causing $>70\%$ slot collisions) while placing local harbor vessels ($3\text{ NM}$ away at $10^\circ$ depression angle) directly inside the collinear's vertical pattern null—solved via $45^\circ$ sectorized Yagi-Uda arrays and downtilted local infill channels.
  - Quantifies two critical shore siting hazards: (1) High-power S/X-band coastal/airport/NEXRAD radars (causing LNA $P_{1\text{dB}}$ compression, gate-junction envelope rectification sweep blanking every $2\text{–}4\text{ s}$, and FET gate-oxide burnout), and (2) North America's continuous $1,000\text{ W}$ **NOAA Weather Radio (NWR)** transmitters at $162.400\text{–}162.550\text{ MHz}$—just **$375\text{ kHz}$ ($0.23\%$) above AIS 2 ($162.025\text{ MHz}$)**—which pass straight through standard $2\text{ MHz}$ SAW filters, saturate 8-bit SDR ADCs, and raise the AIS noise floor by $>70\text{ dB}$ via LO reciprocal mixing unless mitigated by a high-$Q$ ($Q_u \ge 6,000$) quarter-wave coaxial cavity notch filter.
  - Covers at-sea collection platforms (**NOAA NDBC** & Stellwagen Bank buoys with stateful 16-byte Iridium SBD edge decimation, **Liquid Robotics Wave Gliders**, **Saildrone** USVs with Starlink + optical/radar dark-ship cross-cueing, **OceanAero TRITON**, and offshore oil/wind platforms) and catalogs global commercial (**Spire Maritime**, **ORBCOMM**, **Kpler/MarineTraffic**, **S&P Global**, **VesselFinder**, **Pole Star**, **Lloyd's List Intelligence**), community (**AISHub**, **APRS.fi**, **`aisstream.io`**), and government/open scientific (**USCG NAIS**, **Volpe MSSIS**, **NOAA/BOEM `MarineCadastre.gov`**, **EMSA SafeSeaNet**, **Norwegian Kystverket**, **Danish Maritime Authority**) AIS networks.
