# AGENTS.md — `book/part5-navigation-vts`

## Directory Overview

This directory contains **Part V: Navigation, Charts, VTS & Operations** of *AIS: The Open Manual*. It covers the integration of Automatic Identification System (AIS) targets with nautical charts, Electronic Chart Display and Information Systems (ECDIS), Electronic Navigational Charts (ENCs), the IHO S-100 Universal Hydrographic Data Model transition (2026–2029), Vessel Traffic Services (VTS) multi-sensor radar-AIS fusion, landmark historical demonstration programs, government maritime domain awareness (MDA) software stacks, mariner training curricula, Portable Pilot Units (PPUs), and forensic root-cause analyses of AIS-assisted collisions.

## Subdirectories

None. This is a leaf directory within `book/`.

## Files in This Directory

- **`ch21-nautical-charts-ecdis-enc-and-s100.md`** — *Nautical Charts, ECDIS, ENC, S-57, and the IHO S-100 Ecosystem*:
  - **Vertical and Horizontal Datums:** Explains Chart Datum (Lowest Astronomical Tide [LAT], Mean Lower Low Water [MLLW], Mean Low Water [MLW]) for sounded depths versus Highest Astronomical Tide (HAT) / Mean High Water Springs (MHWS) / Mean Higher High Water (MHHW) for bridge vertical clearances; contrasts WGS84 with legacy horizontal datums (NAD27, ED50, Tokyo Datum, local astro datums) where uncompensated datum shifts cause up to $1.5\text{ km}$ discrepancies between GPS-derived AIS positions and legacy chart features.
  - **Scale, `SCAMIN`, and CATZOC:** Defines Compilation Scale (`CSCL`, IHO Usage Bands 1–6), Display Scale (`SCAMIN` clutter suppression vs. `Overscale` stripes), and Category of Zone of Confidence (`CATZOC` `A1`, `A2`, `B`, `C`, `D`, `U`) horizontal/vertical $95\%$ confidence bounds ($U_V = a + b \cdot d$).
  - **Paper Charts vs. RNC vs. ENC vs. ECS vs. ECDIS:** Provides a legal and technical comparison across paper charts, Raster Navigational Charts (RNC, IHO S-61), Electronic Navigational Charts (ENC, IHO S-57 Ed. 3.1 / S-101), non-SOLAS Electronic Chart Systems (ECS, e.g., OpenCPN, Rose Point), and IMO-compliant ECDIS (IMO MSC.232(82) / MSC.530(106), IEC 61174).
  - **IHO S-52 & IEC 62288 AIS Symbology:** Details geometric rendering rules for `AIS01` Sleeping Target triangles, `AIS02` Activated Targets (dashed COG/SOG vector, solid True Heading line, perpendicular Rate of Turn [ROT] barb, and missing-heading cross-bar when `HDG = 511`), Selected target squares, `AIS03` Flashing Red Dangerous Targets ($\text{CPA} \le \text{CPA}_{\text{safe}} \land 0 < \text{TCPA} \le \text{TCPA}_{\text{safe}}$), `AIS05` Slashed Lost Targets, `AIS_OUTLINE` True-Scale Ship Footprints rotated around the Message 5 GNSS Antenna Reference Point $(d_{\text{bow}}, d_{\text{stern}}, d_{\text{port}}, d_{\text{stbd}})$, and SAR Aircraft, AtoN (`◇`), and AIS-SART (`⊕`) icons.
  - **IHO S-100 Universal Hydrographic Data Model (2026–2029 Transition):** Covers IMO Resolution MSC.530(106)/Rev.1 and the suite of S-100 standards—**S-101** (Next-Gen ENC), **S-102** (HDF5 high-resolution gridded bathymetry and uncertainty), **S-104** (water level/tidal heights), **S-111** (surface currents), **S-124** (GML navigational warnings superseding binary `ais-area-notice`), **S-129** (Under-Keel Clearance management), **S-212** (VTS digital service), and **S-421** (`IEC 63173-1` 4D route exchange)—delivered via **VDES (`ITU-R M.2092-1`)** and **IEC 63173-2 (SECOM)** with **IHO S-100 Part 15** AES/ECDSA encryption (replacing S-63 Blowfish/DSA).
  - **Dynamic Under-Keel Clearance (UKC) & Barrass Squat Engine:** Derives $\text{UKC}_{\text{dyn}}(x, y, t) = z_{\text{S-102}}(x, y) - u_{\text{S-102}}(x, y) + \eta_{\text{S-104}}(x, y, t) - (T_{\text{AIS}} + S_{\max}(v) + Z_{\text{wave}})$ using Barrass squat ($S_{\max} = C_b v_{\text{kts}}^2 / 100$ open water or $/ 50$ confined channel), with a runnable Python simulation evaluating true-scale hull corner clearances over a shoal bar.
