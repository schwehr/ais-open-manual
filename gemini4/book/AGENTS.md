# AGENTS.md — `book`

## Directory Overview
The `book/` directory contains the complete manuscript of *The Maritime Automatic Identification System (AIS) Handbook: From RF Physics, Protocol Internals, and Open-Source Software to Global Surveillance, 3D Visualization, Security, and Spatial Analytics* (*AIS: An Open Manual*). It is organized into a master Table of Contents (`README.md`) and 10 subdirectories: Front Matter (`00-front-matter`), Parts I through VIII (`part1-history-governance` through `part8-message-deep-dives`, covering Chapters 1–40), and Appendices A through H (`appendices`).
