# AGENTS.md — `book/part6-software-analytics`

## Directory Overview

This directory contains **Part VI: Software, Post-Processing, 3D Visualization & Spatial Statistics** of *AIS: The Open Manual*. It covers the software engineering, mathematical algorithms, and data science pipelines required to decode raw NMEA 0183 `!AIVDM`/`!AIVDO` streams (`noaadata`, `BitVector`/`bitvector-modern`, `aisparser`, `gpsd`/`AIVDM.txt`, `libais`, `ais-area-notice`, `pyais`, `AIS-catcher`, and Rust/Apache Arrow), scrub and generalize trajectories (`pandas`, `geopandas`, `MovingPandas`, `DuckDB`, `MobilityDB`, `GateHouse IWRAP Mk II`), build sub-millimeter 3D/4D maritime casualty reconstructions in **Blender** (`bpy`), and compute unbiased spatial statistics and trajectory densities.

## Subdirectories

None. This is a leaf directory within `book/`.

  - **4 Historical Generations (`schwehr/gis-history` Lineage):**

- **`ch26-spatial-statistics-and-trajectory-modeling.md`** — *Spatial Statistics and Trajectory Modeling with AIS Data*:
  - **Why Naive "Ping Count Heatmaps" (`COUNT(*)`) Fail Catastrophically:** Formulates the 4-factor expectation integral proving why raw ping counts distort maritime density by up to $1,000\times$:
    1. **ITU-R M.1371 Dynamic Reporting Rate Bias ($90\times$ Skew):** Fast/turning Class A ships broadcast every $2\text{ s}$ ($1,800\text{ pings/hr}$) vs. $180\text{ s}$ ($20\text{ pings/hr}$) for anchored or slow Class B vessels, creating false hotspots at channel bends.
    2. **Spatially Non-Uniform RF Propagation $p_{\text{RF}}(\mathbf{x}, t)$:** Class A ($12.5\text{ W}$ at $49\text{ m}$, $29\text{ NM}$ horizon) vs. Class B ($2\text{ W}$ at $3.2\text{ m}$, $17.4\text{ NM}$ horizon), headland knife-edge diffraction shadows, and seasonal tropospheric ducting spikes.
    3. **VDL Congestion & Satellite Footprint Collision Bias $p_{\text{VDL}}(\mathbf{x}, t)$:** The S-AIS Inverse-Density Paradox, where high vessel counts $N_{\text{FP}}$ inside a $5,000\text{ km}$ LEO footprint exponentially suppress per-ship packet survival $p_{\text{VDL, sat}} \approx \exp(-\alpha\kappa\Lambda_{\text{FP}})$.
    4. **Uneven Shore Networks & Moving Collector Exposure $W(\mathbf{x}, t)$:** Polar-orbiting satellite latitude convergence ($3\times\text{–}5\times$ higher sampling above $60^\circ\text{N/S}$) and patrol aircraft/cutter loiter self-correlation.
  - **Continuous-Time Trajectory Integration & $\Delta t$-Invariance Theorem:** Defines cell residence time $T_i(c)$ ($\text{vessel-hours/km}^2$) and cell distance steamed $L_i(c)$ ($\text{NM/km}^2$), proves **Theorem 26.1** (exact invariance to reporting rate $\Delta t$), and derives the exact $O(K)$ **Amanatides & Woo (1987) parametric grid ray-marching algorithm**.
  - **Horvitz-Thompson Inverse-Probability Weighting & Exposure Normalization:** Shows how to infer empirical burst detection probability $\hat{P}_{\text{det}}^{\text{emp}} = \Delta t_{\text{nominal}}(v, \omega, \text{Class}) / \Delta t_{\text{observed}}$ directly from ITU-R M.1371 reporting schedules, proves **Theorem 26.2** (unbiasedness and variance of the Horvitz-Thompson estimator $\hat{\Lambda}_{\text{HT}}(c)$ with Winsorized floor $\pi_{\min}$ and Hájek stabilization), and derives moving-receiver exposure normalization $\hat{\rho}_{\text{vessels}}(c) = T_{\text{obs}}(c) / (A_c \cdot E(c))$.
  - **Land-Masked Kinematic Brownian Bridges & Equal-Area/H3 Grids:** Derives the **Kinematic Cubic Hermite Brownian Bridge / CTCRW Kalman smoother** conditioned on endpoint positions $(\mathbf{x}_a, \mathbf{x}_b)$ and velocities $(\mathbf{v}_a, \mathbf{v}_b)$ with land-polygon masking; contrasts `EPSG:4326` ($\cos\phi$ error) and `EPSG:3857` ($\sec^2\phi$ error) against equal-area projections (`EPSG:6933`, LAEA) and **Uber H3** hexagonal grids (`h3.cell_area`), with DuckDB SQL and a complete runnable Python reference pipeline.
