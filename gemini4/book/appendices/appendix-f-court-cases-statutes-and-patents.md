> **Purpose:** This appendix provides a unified, cross-referenced legal, regulatory, and intellectual-property index for the Maritime Automatic Identification System (AIS). It is organized into three parts:
> 1. **Part F.1:** Landmark Admiralty Court Decisions, Casualty Investigation Reports, and Sanctions Asset Forfeiture Cases.

## F.3 Comprehensive AIS and Satellite AIS (S-AIS) Patent Index

The intellectual property history of AIS divides into two distinct eras:
1. **First-Generation Terrestrial SOTDMA Patents (1988–2013):** Pioneered by Swedish inventor **Håkan Lans** and assigned to **GP&C Systems International AB**. These patents covered the foundational **Self-Organizing Time Division Multiple Access (STDMA / SOTDMA)** MAC protocol adopted by ITU-R M.1371 and IMO SOLAS Chapter V. Following fierce RAND (Reasonable and Non-Discriminatory) licensing disputes at the ITU, IALA, and IMO in the late 1990s and 2000s, third-party reexamination requests at the US Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO) resulted in the **cancellation of all 19 claims of US Patent 5,506,587 on March 30, 2010**, and the entire first-generation STDMA patent family is now **100% expired and in the public domain worldwide**.
2. **Second-Generation Satellite AIS (S-AIS) De-Collision & VDES Patents (2006–2035):** Filed by spaceborne AIS pioneers (**COM DEV / exactEarth / Spire Global**, **ORBCOMM**, **Kongsberg Seatex / FFI Norway**, **LuxSpace**, and **Saab**) to solve the co-channel time-slot collision, Doppler shift, and differential Time-of-Arrival (TOA) problems encountered when receiving terrestrial SOTDMA signals from Low Earth Orbit.
