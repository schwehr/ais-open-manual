# Appendix E: Master Standards Matrix (ITU-R, IMO, IEC, IALA, IHO, RTCM, NMEA, and CCNR)

> **Purpose:** The Automatic Identification System (AIS) and its next-generation successor, the VHF Data Exchange System (VDES), are governed by more than 80 interdependent international treaties, radiocommunication recommendations, hardware type-approval specifications, digital bus protocols, hydrographic display rules, and regional inland waterway mandates. This appendix provides an exhaustive, cross-referenced engineering matrix of every standard that defines or impacts AIS, linked directly to the chapters of this handbook where each specification is analyzed in depth.

---
