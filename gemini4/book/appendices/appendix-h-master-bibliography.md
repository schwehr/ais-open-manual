# Appendix H: Master Bibliography and Verified Citation Index

> **Purpose:** This appendix compiles the complete, verified master bibliography for *The Maritime Automatic Identification System (AIS) Handbook* across all 30 chapters and Appendices A through G. Every entry includes its canonical BibTeX citation key (cross-referenced to [`MASTER_BIBLIOGRAPHY.bib`](../../MASTER_BIBLIOGRAPHY.bib)), full bibliographic metadata, Digital Object Identifier (DOI) or permanent archival URL, and the handbook chapters in which it is cited.
>
> **Organization by Domain:**
> * **H.1:** Historical Lineage, GIS History, and Global Fishing / Dark-Vessel Studies
> * **H.2:** Primary International Standards (ITU-R, ITU-T, 3GPP, IMO, IEC, IALA, IHO, RTCM, NMEA, CCNR, WMO)
> * **H.3:** Open-Source AIS Decoders, Post-Processing Libraries, and 3D Visualization (`libais`, `noaadata`, `ais-area-notice`, `gpsd`, `aisparser`, `pyais`, `AIS-catcher`, Rust Crates, `MovingPandas`, `Blender`, `BlenderGIS`, `DuckDB`)
> * **H.4:** RF Propagation Physics, Satellite AIS (S-AIS) De-Collision, VDL Loading, and Passive Radar
> * **H.5:** Cybersecurity, Spoofing, GNSS Jamming, RF Fingerprinting (SEI), and Telecom Signaling (`SS7` / `Diameter`)
> * **H.6:** Marine Mammal Conservation (**Listen for Whales / Whale Alert**), Ship Emissions (`STEAM`), and Underwater Radiated Noise (`URN`)
> * **H.7:** Commodity Trading, Bloomberg Terminal Analytics, and Maritime Economics
> * **H.8:** Landmark Admiralty Court Cases, Maritime Casualties, Sanctions Forfeitures, Statutes, and AIS Patents
