# Directory Summary: `book/part4-space-air-vdes`

## Overview
`book/part4-space-air-vdes` contains **Part IV: Spaceborne, Airborne, Specialized Devices, and AIS 2.0 / VDES (Chapters 17–20)** of *The Open Manual of AIS*. This directory covers Low Earth Orbit (LEO) Satellite AIS (S-AIS) orbital geometry, multi-cell SOTDMA de-collision DSP, single-satellite Doppler+TOA geolocation verification, and spaceborne VHF transmission; airborne AIS transmission (Message 9), manned/unmanned maritime ISR aircraft and drones, emergency SAR beacons (`AIS-SART`, `AIS-MOB`, `EPIRB-AIS`), and VHF Radio Direction Finding (RDF/DF); commercial fishing gear AIS net buoys, the ITU-R M.2135-0 Autonomous Maritime Radio Device (AMRD) framework, and unintended user hacks; and the next-generation VHF Data Exchange System (**VDES / AIS 2.0**, ITU-R M.2092-1).

    5. *Opportunistic Passive Bistatic Radar (PBR) & Bragg Sea-Clutter Sensing:* Correlating direct AIS reference bursts against scattered echoes via the Cross-Ambiguity Function $\chi(\tau, f_d)$ to detect non-emitting ("dark") vessels and measure ocean wave spectra.
  * **Automated Anomaly Classifier:** Includes `detect_fishing_gear_and_hacks.py` projecting targets into local ENU meters to classify compliant AMRD-B (`979`), illegal net buoys, mother fishing vessels, and Message 5 `Destination` hacks.

* **Topic:** AIS 2.0 (VDES): What Is It, Is It Real, and How Does It Work?
* **Key Contents:**
  * **Reality, Legal Status, & Why Legacy AIS Hit a Wall:** Documents that **VDES (ITU-R M.2092-1)** is operational in LEO (**Sternula-1**, **NorSat-TD**, **Ymir-1**) and coastal testbeds today and enters mandatory **IMO SOLAS Chapter V** legal force on **January 1, 2028**, solving legacy AIS's 4 architectural bottlenecks (VDL slot congestion $>80\%$, multi-slot binary bloat without FEC, inability to deliver $50\text{ KB}+$ IHO S-100 products, and zero cryptographic authentication).
  * **The 4-Subsystem Architecture & Frequency Plan (ITU-R M.2092-1):**
    1. *Subsystem 1 — Legacy AIS:* **AIS 1** ($161.975\text{ MHz}$) & **AIS 2** ($162.025\text{ MHz}$) at $9.6\text{ kbps}$ GMSK (plus Ch 75/76 LR-AIS), protected at highest priority.
    2. *Subsystem 2 — VDES ASM:* **ASM 1** (Ch 2027, $161.950\text{ MHz}$) & **ASM 2** (Ch 2028, $162.000\text{ MHz}$) using $\pi/4\text{-QPSK}$ + Turbo FEC at **$19.2\text{ kbps}$** ($2\times$ AIS) to offload binary messages.
    3. *Subsystem 3 — VDE-TER (Terrestrial Wideband):* Channels 1024–1086 ($157.1875\text{–}157.3375\text{ MHz}$ ship-to-shore) and 2024–2086 ($161.7875\text{–}161.9375\text{ MHz}$ shore-to-ship/ship-to-ship) bonding $25/50/100\text{ kHz}$ channels with adaptive $\pi/4\text{-QPSK}$, 8-PSK, and **16-QAM** + Turbo/LDPC FEC up to **$307.2\text{ kbps}$** ($32\times$ AIS).
    4. *Subsystem 4 — VDE-SAT (Bidirectional LEO Satellite):* Dedicated **$525\text{ kHz}$ downlink block ($160.9625\text{–}161.4875\text{ MHz}$)** governed by an elevation-dependent ITU Power Flux Density (PFD) mask, paired with spread-spectrum/QPSK uplinks.
  * **PHY Framing, Turbo FEC, R-Mode CRLB, & PKI / IHO S-100 SECOM:** Dissects the 512-bit/256-symbol VDES ASM slot layout (ramp, 27-symbol sync word, Link ID MCS header, Rate $1/2$ or $3/4$ 3GPP Turbo FEC + block interleaving, and **CRC-32**), derives the **Cramér-Rao Lower Bound (CRLB)** proving why $100\text{ kHz}$ VDE-TER R-Mode ($\beta_{\text{rms}} \approx 23.1\text{ kHz}$) achieves $>10\times$ finer TOA ranging precision ($\sigma_r \approx 0.56\text{ m}$ at $10\text{ dB}$ SNR) than $25\text{ kHz}$ AIS R-Mode ($\beta_{\text{rms}} \approx 2.15\text{ kHz}$), and explains **Maritime Connectivity Platform (MCP)** X.509/MRN PKI + 64-byte **ECDSA** signatures under **IEC 63173-2 (SECOM)** and **IHO S-100 Part 15**, with a runnable Python capacity, FER, S-100 transfer-time ($2.64\text{ s}$ vs. $3{,}330\text{ s}$), and R-Mode CRLB benchmark.
